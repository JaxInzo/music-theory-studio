MusicIsEasy — Remove mistakes automatically

Upload your raw voiceover and your script. The app transcribes your audio, finds every mistake and repeated phrase, picks the best take of each, and exports a clean seamless audio file.

How to record for best results
⏸️
Pause 1-2 seconds before restarting
When you make a mistake, stop completely. Wait 1-2 seconds of silence, then restart the sentence from the beginning.
🔁
Restart the full sentence
Always go back to the start of the sentence you messed up — not mid-phrase. This gives a clean complete take to work with.
🎯
Don't talk over mistakes
Stop speaking completely before restarting. Silence then restart is the golden rule for clean cuts.
✅
Last take wins if equal
If two takes are equally good, the app always picks the later one — you naturally improve as you go.
