How to record for best results
⏸️
Pause 1-2 seconds before restarting
When you make a mistake, stop completely. Wait 1-2 seconds of silence, then restart the sentence from the beginning. That silence is the cut point.
🔁
Restart the full sentence
Always go back to the start of the sentence you messed up — not mid-phrase. This gives the cleaner a clean, complete take to work with.
🎯
Don't talk over mistakes
Stop speaking completely before restarting. Overlapping speech is hard to cut cleanly. Silence then restart is the golden rule.
✅
Last take wins if equal
If two takes are equally good, the app always picks the later one — you naturally get better as you go, so the last attempt is usually the cleanest.
